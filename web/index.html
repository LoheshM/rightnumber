Wrong number?
Not on our watch.

Scammers plant “customer care” numbers in Google results and Maps pins. Before you dial, RightNumber reads what Google and Maps show you, finds the brand's own website, and tells you whether that site prints the number — and which numbers it does print.

  • 4 live searches per check, every one shown
  • Brand's own pages read, not directories
  • Never says “safe” — only what the brand prints
